Why this work

I help people stop guessing about their own next move.

Most of us aren't stuck because we lack options — we're stuck because we've lost the thread on what we actually want. My work is to help you find that thread again: through structured conversation, honest reflection, and a bit of accountability.

Whether you're rethinking your career, sitting with a big decision, or just feeling foggy about your own direction, we start from where you actually are — not where you think you should be.
